# MRI Lead Set

### MRI Lead Set of the IT’IS Foundation

The MRI-optimized electrode lead set is designed for safe, artifact-free magnetic resonance imaging scans. Each set includes ten electrodes and uses novel lead technology with a distributed resistance of 3 ± 10% kΩ/m.

|    |            |
|:-----------|:------------|
|Number of Electrodes per Set|10 |
|Electrode Type|Ag/AgCl; others upon request |
|Electrode Lead Type|Novel lead technology with distributed resistance of 3+/-10%&nbsp;kOhm/m warranting safe and artifact-free MRI scans |
|Electrode Lead Length|60 / 80 cm |
|Electrode Connector Type|Touch-Proof 1.5 mm male |
