# Resistive Leads

### Novel Technology

The novel resistive lead technology was developed by TI Solutions in collaboration with the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology [(EMPA)](https://www.empa.ch/web/empa/), Switzerland, and features optimized distributed resistance of 4.4 kΩ/m ± 20%. It is the most effective solution for minimization of radiofrequency-induced currents in cables across many applications, enabling, for example, safe and artifact-free temporal interference stimulation during functional MRI (fMRI).

### Applications 

The novel lead technology provides versatile solutions for a wide range of applications involving MRI:
* Closed-loop neuromodulation studies that combine stimulation and electrophysiology with fMRI
* Electroencephalography performed simultaneously with fMRI
* Electrocardiography monitoring during fMRI
* Electromyography with fMRI for motor-function studies
* Electro-oculography monitoring for sleep and vision studies
* Electrodermal activity (galvanic skin response) monitoring
* MRI-compatible polysomnography and sleep research
* Peripheral nerve electrophysiology
* Intracranial electrophysiology
* Simultaneous electrical stimulation and electrophysiology recording during fMRI
* Multimodal brain-body monitoring during fMRI
* General low-noise biopotential recording in high-field MRI environments
