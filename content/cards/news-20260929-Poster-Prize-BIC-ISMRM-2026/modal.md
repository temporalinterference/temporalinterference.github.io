# Outstanding Poster Award at BIC-ISMRM 2026

Congratulations to Anna Sadilova, PhD student at King’s College London, who received 1st Place for Outstanding Poster Presentation at the 31st BIC-ISMRM Annual Scientific Meeting, held September 2–4, 2026, in London, UK.

Anna’s award-winning work, “Assessment of temporal interference stimulation safety concurrent 7T MRI,” investigated the compatibility, thermal safety and imaging quality of combining temporal interference (TI) stimulation with 7T magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) in healthy volunteers. The study was conducted as part of Dr. Inês Violante’s group at KCL’s School of Biomedical Engineering & Imaging Sciences. It used our TIBS-R device together with the 7T TI-MRI Filter Solution, including dedicated leads developed for artifact-free functional MRI recording.

The results support the safe use of TI stimulation during 7T MRI and open further opportunities for high-resolution studies of stimulation effects in the human brain.

Congratulations, Anna, on this well-deserved recognition!
