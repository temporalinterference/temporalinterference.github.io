# TIP Clinic
## What is TIP Clinic?

TIP Clinic translates personalized TI planning from research into everyday clinical practice.

This medical device-certified software is designed for the high-precision TIBS-HOME Series and builds on the proven technologies and extensive user feedback of TIP Research, developed by the IT’IS Foundation. It combines the validated planning engine of TIP Research with a streamlined, clinician-oriented workflow that requires no computational modeling expertise.

Starting from patient magnetic resonance images, TIP Clinic automatically generates a personalized head model and guides clinicians through every step of the treatment planning process – from electrode placement and current optimization to the quantitative evaluation of TI metrics at the target brain location – all within an intuitive, easy-to-use interface.
